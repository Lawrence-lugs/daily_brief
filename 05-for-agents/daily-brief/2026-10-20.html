256-Element Analog CIM Beamforming Accelerator Achieves 20.5-dB PSLR at 414 mW in 28 nm

A test chip implements a Slepian beamforming accelerator using analog compute-in-memory for complex-valued vector-matrix multiplication, projecting 256 I/Q inputs into a low-dimensional subspace. The 28-nm CMOS chip delivers 20.5-dB peak-to-sidelobe ratio over 280-MHz bandwidth while consuming 414 mW, showcasing analog CIM for massive MIMO.

Silicon-proven analog CIM for beamforming with competitive efficiency, relevant to accel and CIM research.
