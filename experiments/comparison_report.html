Key Observations

  1. Different philosophies: Keyword balances subtopics + source types; LR optimizes p(like) within interest clusters.
  2. LR heavily favors papers: Weight +0.706 for researchlike, -0.551 for newslike (reflects like history).
  3. Guaranteed interest coverage: Round-robin across 5 core clusters ensures every interest appears daily.
  4. Very low overlap: Jaccard ~0.03 (primary), 0.00 (science) - engines pick almost disjoint sets.
  5. Freshness: LR picks fresher items on average (3.1 vs 6.5 days) due to value function V = p * exp(-lambda*A) * L(days).
  6. Explore behavior: Both explore at ~26% (epsilon=0.25), but LR explore picks have very low V values.
  7. Simulation stability: 30.5% of pool eventually picked, consecutive Jaccard 0.45 = good variety.
