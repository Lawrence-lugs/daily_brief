Monolithic 3D DRAM PNM architecture targets generative normalizing flows

M3D-FLOW integrates a Cu-Cu hybrid-bonded logic die with monolithic 3D DRAM to eliminate memory bottlenecks in normalizing flow models. The architecture exploits dense bank integration and a tiered organization to achieve 4.2× throughput and 3.8× energy efficiency over GPU baselines on image generation workloads.

Shows 3D-stacked PNM can accelerate memory-bound generative models beyond GPUs.

dseplacementgpu

GPU-accelerated differentiable RMST wirelength model for global placement

DiffRMST provides a fully differentiable rectilinear minimum spanning tree model that captures multi-pin net topology better than HPWL while remaining polynomial-time. Implemented in CUDA, it enables gradient-based placement optimization with 12% wirelength reduction over HPWL on standard benchmarks at 5× runtime overhead.

Gives DSE a practical, differentiable wirelength proxy for analytical placement.

SIK3 kinase controls synaptic NMDA receptors via GluN1 phosphorylation

Nature Neuroscience reports that SIK3 phosphorylates GluN1 at a single site to recruit NMDA receptors to synapses. Loss of SIK3 sharply reduces synaptic NMDARs, while a gain-of-function mutation increases them.

Mechanistic insight into synaptic plasticity, relevant to neuromorphic hardware models.

Complex-momentum form factors from lattice QCD via bilateral Laplace transform

A bilateral Laplace transform applied to position-space Euclidean correlators yields form factors at complex momenta directly, avoiding analytic continuation of correlators or inverse problems; demonstrated for the pion form factor in QCD.
